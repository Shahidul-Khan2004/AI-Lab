**Practice Problems**

Instructions: Take input from the user. Use the loop or slicing technique specified in each question. Match the sample output format. Solutions are not included.
## Problem 1
Take an integer. Print whether it is positive, negative, or zero; whether it is even or odd (if nonzero); and its square.

Sample Input: -6

Sample Output: Negative Number Even Number Square: 36
## Problem 2
Take a positive integer n. Using a for loop, find the sum and count of even numbers and odd numbers from 1 through n.

Sample Input: 10

Sample Output: Even Sum: 30 Odd Sum: 25 Even Count: 5 Odd Count: 5
## Problem 3
Take an integer n and print its multiplication table from 1 through 10 using a for loop.

Sample Input: 5

Sample Output: 5 x 1 = 5 5 x 2 = 10 ... 5 x 10 = 50
## Problem 4
Take a nonnegative integer n. Calculate n! using a while loop. Assume 0! = 1.

Sample Input: 5

Sample Output: Factorial: 120
## Problem 5
Take a positive integer. Using a while loop, count its digits, calculate the sum of its digits, and print its last digit.

Sample Input: 4532

Sample Output: Total Digits: 4 Sum of Digits: 14 Last Digit: 2
## Problem 6
Take marks of 6 subjects and store them in a list. Print the complete list, first 3 and last 3 marks using slicing, total, average (2 decimal places), and count of subjects with marks ≥ 50.

Sample Input: 75, 40, 85, 60, 45, 90

Sample Output: First 3: [75, 40, 85] Last 3: [60, 45, 90] Total: 395 Average: 65.83 Passed Subjects: 4
## Problem 7
Take 8 integers into a list. Using a loop and if-else statements, count positive numbers, negative numbers, and zeros.

Sample Input: 4, -2, 0, 7, -5, 0, 9, -1

Sample Output: Positive: 3 Negative: 3 Zero: 2
## Problem 8
Take a list of 8 integers. Using slicing, print the first 4 elements, last 4 elements, every second element starting at index 0, and the reversed list.

Sample Input: 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 70, 80

Sample Output: First 4: [10, 20, 30, 40] Last 4: [50, 60, 70, 80] Alternate: [10, 30, 50, 70] Reversed: [80, 70, 60, 50, 40, 30, 20, 10]
## Problem 9
Take 5 integers into a list. Using a for loop (without max() or min()), find the largest, smallest, and their difference.

Sample Input: 12, 45, 7, 30, 18

Sample Output: Largest: 45 Smallest: 7 Difference: 38
## Problem 10
Take a string containing only English letters. Using a loop, count vowels (a, e, i, o, u) and consonants; print the reversed string using slicing.

Sample Input: Programming

Sample Output: Vowels: 3 Consonants: 8 Reversed: gnimmargorP
## Problem 11
Take a positive integer n. Use nested for loops to print a right-angled star triangle with n rows.

Sample Input: 5

Sample Output: * ** *** **** *****
## Problem 12
Take a positive integer n. Use nested loops to print an inverted star triangle with n rows.

Sample Input: 5

Sample Output: ***** **** *** ** *
## Problem 13
Take a positive integer n. Use nested loops to print increasing sequences from 1 to the row number.

Sample Input: 5

Sample Output: 1 12 123 1234 12345
## Problem 14
Take a positive integer n. Use nested loops to print each row number repeated that many times.

Sample Input: 5

Sample Output: 1 22 333 4444 55555
## Problem 15
Take the number of products n, then input each product price using a loop and store prices in a list. Calculate the total using a loop and count prices greater than 500 taka. Apply discounts: total below 1000 → 0%; 1000 to below 3000 → 5%; 3000 or above → 10%. Print total, count, discount amount, and final bill.

Sample Input: Number of Products: 4 Prices: 500, 800, 1200, 600

Sample Output: Total Bill: 3100 Products Above 500: 3 Discount: 310 Final Bill: 2790
